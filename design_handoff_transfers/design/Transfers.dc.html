6 Utility pass, with mode icons

Corner registration marks are gone. Card and field edges are solid 1.5 px at higher contrast, so they hold in sun. Each bus header is a reversed band, so the next bus is findable without reading. Countdowns and transfer minutes are heavier. Every route chip carries its mode icon (the 5a set from Mode Icons). The bus segment of each time bar takes the route color. On wide screens, transfer points sit side by side and saved searches tile. Each train row and bus card has one spoken summary for screen readers, including the BART line color. The prototype and states below use this version.
